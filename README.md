# Calm

A small, private app for calming down and working through triggers. Save it to your iPhone home screen and it works like an app, even offline.

- **I need to calm down**: one minute of guided breathing, then 5-4-3-2-1 grounding.
- **Work through a trigger**: pick the fear underneath, then go through short questions about the thought, your body and your younger self. Quick (about 5 min) or full (about 20 min).
- **Journal**: write freely (or dictate with the keyboard microphone), tag a mood, and look back at past reflections. Includes backup and restore.

## Privacy

Everything you write is stored only on your phone, in the app's local storage. This repository holds only the blank app. There is no server, no account and no tracking, and nothing you type is ever sent to GitHub or anywhere else.

## Install on iPhone

1. Open the site in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Always open Calm from the home screen icon. Deleting the icon deletes your entries, so use **About → Save backup** now and then.

*This is a self-care tool, not therapy. If you are in crisis, call your local emergency number (US: call or text 988).*
